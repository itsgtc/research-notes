Cloud Security Assessment: Q3 2026 Summary

Executive Summary

This quarter's assessment covered 142 cloud workloads across three major providers. Overall security posture improved by 12% compared to Q2, driven primarily by improved IAM configurations and network segmentation controls.

Key Finding 1: Storage Bucket Misconfiguration

23 of 142 workloads (16.2%) had at least one publicly accessible storage bucket. This represents a decrease from 24.1% in Q2.

Key Finding 2: Overprivileged Service Accounts

Service account privileges remain the highest-risk area, with 67% of accounts having permissions exceeding their operational requirements.
